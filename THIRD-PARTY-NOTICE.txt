GSAP (GreenSock Animation Platform) is loaded from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/gsap@3.15.0/dist/gsap.min.js when Motion Button is active on an Elementor page.
GSAP is provided by GreenSock under its Standard No Charge License: https://gsap.com/standard-license/
GSAP is not bundled into this ZIP. If the CDN is unavailable, Motion Button uses a limited native Web Animations fallback for text motion.
